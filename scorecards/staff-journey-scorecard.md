# Staff and advisor journey scorecard

Use the same fictional inn report as the [owner journey](owner-journey-scorecard.md). Begin with an authorized staff account, a user without staff access, and a report that is still being prepared. Complete the staff review before the owner requests a consultation. Staff accounts cannot self-register: provision them at boot with `STAFF_EMAILS` and `STAFF_PASSWORD`, and note that the roster skips an email that already belongs to an owner account.

See the [project overview](../PROJECT.md) for staff responsibilities and report access. Use the result labels in the [MVP success criteria](mvp-success-criteria-and-agent-scorecards.md). Record whether notifications and report generation used live services or a labeled substitute.

### Handoff notes

- Roles used: owner accounts were created through signup by the test harnesses (their reports cover the pending, ready and consulted states), and the designated staff account was provisioned at boot from `STAFF_EMAILS`/`STAFF_PASSWORD` set inside the harnesses only — staff accounts cannot self-register, and the roster skips an email that already belongs to an owner account.
- The ready notification and report access checks (S1, S2) ran against the labeled substitutes: the local mail driver `server/mail/local.ts` (`.eml` outbox, no live email env locally) and the on-page-labeled rule-based report draft. Live email delivery stays Unverified; no other live provider is involved in the staff path.
- The advisor finds the request through the staff report list: `/staff` (backed by `GET /api/staff/reports`) marks the report with its consultation timestamp next to the business and owner contact details, and the ready staff mail points to the same list.
- Run evidence: `npm run build`; `node --test tests/*.test.mjs` (100 pass, 0 fail); `node tests/browser-journey.mjs --strict` (40 passed, 0 pending, 0 failed, including `staff.interview-notice`).
